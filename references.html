Where the look comes from, the typefaces, and what the site is made with.

The drawings

The illustrations follow my own art style: heavy navy linework, flat shading, halftone dots and a white sticker edge around each piece. The lines are drawn as chains of small beads that shift a little a few times a second, so the drawings feel hand-made and alive. The movement stops if your device is set to reduce motion, or with Pause motion below.

Typefaces

Red Hat Display for headings, Manrope for text and Red Hat Mono for code, all under the SIL Open Font License.

The pictures

Every project picture is the real work: the finished pieces, photographed or exported from the files I delivered.

How it's built

Plain HTML and CSS generated from one data file of projects, with no framework, deployed from GitHub. The Personal Site Build case study explains how.
