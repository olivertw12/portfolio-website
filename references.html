Where the look comes from

The deep blue water, the shafts of light and the glowing white type follow the stage screen and tour poster for LE SSERAFIM’s 2026 PUREFLOW tour. Both are reference material only; nothing from them appears on the site. The tour’s own artwork is at pureflowtour2026.com.

Water, light and sand

Nothing in the background is a photograph or a video. The water column, the light from the surface, the drifting particles and the seabed are drawn live in the browser.

Glass and the anchor

The anchor and the three section icons are shaped from this site’s own vector marks. The anchor hangs from a simulated chain and re-forms into each section’s mark as that work comes up, so its sway, its landing and the sand it raises are worked out as you scroll.

Typefaces
