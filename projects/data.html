Save the Data

Posters for a ransomware tabletop exercise

Client
DATA Club & Tech Association of Iowa
Role
President & Lead Designer
Year
2025
Deliverables
Flyers, Social, Signage

As President of the DATA Club, I partnered with the Tech Association of Iowa to run a tabletop ransomware exercise, showing students and industry professionals how businesses respond to a cybersecurity crisis.

The workshop was a three-hour morning session, so it needed a clear visual theme to draw people in and stand apart from ordinary academic lectures.

I created a retro-computing style with pixelated icons and distressed typography, and made two colorways: System Blue for light mode and Critical Pink for dark mode. The two versions were hung side by side in the science halls.

The posters promoted the workshop across campus to students and technology professionals.
