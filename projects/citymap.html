Shorewood City Map

An illustrated play rug of the city

Client
Community Prototype
Role
Illustrator
Year
2024
Deliverables
Rug Design, Map

This began as a university project to design a working prototype that would benefit a local community. I wanted to make something that would help younger residents learn their city and feel at home in it.

A map children can play on

City layouts are abstract for children when they only see them on a paper map or a screen. I wanted to turn Shorewood’s geography into something they could explore through play.

Roads wide enough for toy cars

I designed an illustrated rug that works as a large play mat, with a stylized but accurate map of Shorewood. Landmarks are drawn as distinct icons, and the roads are wide enough to drive toy cars along.

A finished rug prototype

The finished prototype shows Shorewood, its real landmarks and the surrounding townships, and sits somewhere between a toy and a learning tool.
