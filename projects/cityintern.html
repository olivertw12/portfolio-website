Shorewood Internship

Fillable city forms on the city brand

Client
City of Shorewood
Role
Graphic Design Intern
Year
2024
Deliverables
Fillable Forms, PDFs

The City of Shorewood runs many administrative processes that depend on documents, from permits to internal HR forms. I joined as a Graphic Design Intern to help bring its materials up to date.

Many of the existing documents were outdated, inconsistent in appearance and hard to fill in. My task was to review them and rebuild them as one consistent set that followed the Shorewood Branding Kit.

I used InDesign and Adobe Acrobat to redesign the documents as fillable PDFs, applying the brand kit throughout and adding form fields, dropdown menus and clearer instructions.

The city received a library of updated digital forms with consistent formatting and interactive fields, which also cut down on paper.
