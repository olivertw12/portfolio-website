Duolingo × Rocket League

Campaign concept for the Young Ones competition

Client
Young Ones Competition
Role
Illustration
Year
2024
Deliverables
Deck, Video, Illustration

Our team made this campaign for the One Club for Creativity’s Young Ones competition, which asks students to develop marketing strategies that help global brands reach specific goals. We responded to Duolingo’s brief.

Duolingo wanted to reach a new audience. We chose gamers, whose play depends on communicating across languages, and picked Rocket League for its large international player base.

We pitched an in-game crossover event. I led the visual development in Adobe Illustrator, combining assets from Duolingo’s brand kit with the look of Rocket League.

We presented the concept as a campaign deck and a video pitch.
