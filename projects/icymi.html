ICYMI Podcast

Cover art and weekly posters for a campus podcast

Client
Student Media
Role
Co-Host & Designer
Year
2022
Deliverables
Cover Art, Weekly Posters

ICYMI was a weekly news podcast covering Drake University and the Des Moines area. I was a co-host and the lead designer.

A podcast is audio only, which makes it hard to notice on a physical campus. The identity had to work as a tiny thumbnail on Spotify and as a poster on a crowded bulletin board.

I designed a bold cover to anchor the brand, then built a poster template that could be updated for each week’s episode, so new promotion could be made quickly.

The cover and weekly posters promoted the show across campus.
