Pyrkia Health

Logo for a nature-focused health program

Client
Pyrkia Health Services
Role
Logo Design
Year
2024
Deliverables
Logo, Brand Assets

Pyrkia Health Services is a nature-focused wellness program. Its aim is to connect physical health with the natural world and with a sense of unity between a person and their surroundings.

Health and wellness branding is crowded with generic imagery. The client wanted a mark that read as care and unity, felt organic and human, and avoided the usual medical clichés.

I built the logo around an abstract human figure, standing for the person within the natural world, set within a prominent circle that stands for completeness and the cycle of health.

The final mark joins the figure and the circle into one symbol, shown here on Pyrkia’s website.
