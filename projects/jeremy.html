Jeremy Messersmith

Flyers for a songwriting workshop and performance

Client
Becky Hoffmann Mgmt
Role
Art Direction & Design
Year
2026
Deliverables
Digital Posters (1:1, 4:5)

Jeremy Messersmith was hosting two related events in February 2026: a free songwriting workshop and a songwriter circle performance. Each needed its own digital flyer, and the two had to feel like a pair while making it clear they were different events.

I wanted the flyers to feel inviting and handmade, to match the intimate scale of the events and the whimsy that runs through Jeremy’s work.

I chose a scrapbook collage style to echo the hands-on nature of a songwriting workshop, and gave the workshop and the show different color palettes to tell them apart. The cats in the collage are a nod to his song “Everybody Gets a Kitten.”

The flyers were used on social media and ticketing platforms for both events.
