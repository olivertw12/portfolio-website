Dessa East Coast Tour

Tour announcement artwork for Instagram

Client
Becky Hoffmann Mgmt
Role
Graphic Design
Year
2026
Deliverables
Social Assets (4:5, 9:16)

Dessa was announcing a March 2026 tour with stops in Boston, Philadelphia, Brooklyn and Washington, D.C. Her management needed a set of artwork sized for Instagram feeds and stories to support the announcement.

The direction changed partway through. My first concept used moody photography, and the client felt it was too somber for the energy of the tour. The new goal was to match the pink tones of the cover of her recent EP.

I recreated that pink color grade by hand so the tour artwork would sit alongside the EP. To suit the new tone, I chose a fresh font pairing that kept the atmosphere while keeping the dates and cities easy to read.

The team approved the revised designs, and they were used for the tour announcement in mid-January 2026.
