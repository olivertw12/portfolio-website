Mixed Media Studies

Most of my work is digital design and data, but I keep a practice in traditional drawing. These studies were made between 2021 and 2024.

Traditional media has no undo. With charcoal and ink, every mark stays on the page, so each stroke needs commitment and a real understanding of the material.

I work mostly in charcoal and pastel. These studies recreate form under high-contrast light, using value and texture to build volume.

The practice keeps my fundamentals sharp, and the discipline of drawing carries straight into my digital work, especially my eye for composition and balance.
