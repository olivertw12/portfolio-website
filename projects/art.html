Background

While my professional work focuses on digital design and analytics, I maintain a dedicated practice in traditional fine art. This collection represents a compilation of studies created between 2021 and 2024.

02

The Challenge

The challenge in traditional media is the lack of an 'undo' button. Working with charcoal and ink requires commitment to every stroke and a deep understanding of physical materials.

03

The Solution

I primarily work with a variety of charcoals and pastels. My studies focus on recreating form in high-contrast lighting scenarios, relying on value and texture to communicate volume and emotion.

04

The Result

This ongoing practice keeps my fundamental artistic skills sharp. The discipline required for traditional drawing directly translates to my digital work, improving my eye for composition and balance.
